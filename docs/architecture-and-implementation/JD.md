Why This Role Exists
PubX builds next-generation publisher-first agentic advertising infrastructure. Our AI makes real-time, revenue-critical decisions for digital publishers. Our Bid Intelligence uses machine learning to optimize every programmatic ad auction individually, generating measurable revenue uplift for publishers. We’re currently ranked #5 globally in Prebid Analytics Adapter Rankings, and growing.
The problem we’re solving
Digital publishers leave significant revenue on the table because their tools are are still largely manual, static or simple rule-based. Every ad impression is unique, but most advertising systems treat them the same. PubX's AI analyzes bid-stream data and historical patterns to arrange optimal deals, in near real-time.
As a founding member of AgenticAdvertising.org, we're building the next generation of autonomous advertising infrastructure.
